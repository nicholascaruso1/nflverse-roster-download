## Cross-checks and limitations

The NFL schedule archive can calculate all-Thursday records and Thursday kickoffs at or after 7 PM, but it does not identify the branded TNF broadcast package. Those schedule proxies must not be reported as verified TNF records. The claims of 3-9 and 4-8 therefore remain external claims pending a broadcast-level source.

The elapsed-month calculation must be distinguished from completed calendar months and exact elapsed days. October 13, 2022 to November 12, 2026 is 49 calendar-month boundaries but is one day short of 49 completed months.

Pythagorean values are sums under the repeating 1–9 mapping. They are not the same thing as digital roots of A1Z26 ordinal values.

## Interpretation

These are competing, descriptive numerical narratives. They do not establish which team will win. Calendar and cipher matches are vulnerable to selection effects, correlated transformations, and multiple comparisons. Any predictive claim requires a frozen feature definition and chronological out-of-sample testing.

**Governance:** No odds or market data used. No outcome inferred from the narrative.
